About BHATI LAW ASSOCIATES

BHATI LAW ASSOCIATES is a full-service law firm committed to delivering effective, result-oriented, and client-focused legal solutions. With a foundation built on integrity, professionalism, and deep legal expertise, the firm offers comprehensive legal services across a broad spectrum of practice areas, including civil and criminal litigation, corporate and commercial law, constitutional law, family law, property disputes, arbitration, and legal consultancy.

Our team of experienced advocates and legal consultants is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of legal ethics while providing strategic counsel and robust representation before all levels of courts and tribunals.

  • Representation before Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRT) and NCLT
  • Execution of decrees and enforcement of judgments
  • All SARFAESI related work including possession
