Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Personal details (name, email, phone number) when you submit a contact form or book an appointment
  • Case-related information you share during consultations
  • Website usage data through cookies and analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and manage appointments
  • Provide legal services and consultation
  • Send relevant legal updates (with your consent)
  • Improve our website and services

3. Data Protection

We take the security of your personal data seriously. All information shared with us, including case details, is protected by attorney-client privilege and stored securely. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law, with your explicit consent, or as necessary to provide our legal services.

5. Attorney-Client Privilege

All communications between you and our attorneys are protected by attorney-client privilege. We maintain strict confidentiality of all case-related information in accordance with the Advocates Act, 1961 and Bar Council of India rules.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. These cookies do not contain personal information and are used solely for analytics and website functionality.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
